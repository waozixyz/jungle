# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Play Jungle Academy in your browser or on Linux, in English, German, or Spanish.
- Choose from nine illustrated books with 101 words, including home rooms and nature.
- Hear each word once in Simple mode, or practice the word and its letters in Spelling mode.
- Finish each book and continue automatically to the next one.
- Enjoy a watercolor book shelf, clearer animal counting, and new dinosaur shapes and scenery.
- Draw, color, save pictures, write stories, and print without dinosaur voiceovers.

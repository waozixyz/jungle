# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- Picture books fill the window and adapt to portrait, landscape, phones, tablets and desktop resizing.
- Browse large book covers without a title or category headings above them.
- Add a Vegetables book with sixteen pictures and English, German and Spanish words and narration.
- Use touch buttons to answer counting questions and the phone keyboard to write dinosaur stories.
- Dino Colors draws its scenes and dinosaurs as smooth line art instead of blocky pixels.
- See the dinosaur you are placing inside a clear box you can drag, and mirror it with a button.
- Find every Dino Colors tool by its icon, with tools below the picture on tall screens.

## 0.1.0

- Play Jungle Academy in your browser or on Linux, in English, German, or Spanish.
- Choose from nine illustrated books with 101 words, including home rooms and nature.
- Hear each word once in Simple mode, or practice the word and its letters in Spelling mode.
- Finish each book and continue automatically to the next one.
- Browse a scrollable shelf grouped into animals and nature, food, and home rooms.
- Open a larger desktop window and enjoy clearer animal counting and new dinosaur shapes and scenery.
- Turn browser book pages immediately while narration loads in the background.
- Draw, color, save pictures, write stories, and print without dinosaur voiceovers.
